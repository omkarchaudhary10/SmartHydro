IoT-Based Smart Hydroponic Farming System with Automatic Water Circulation

A soil-free farming system using IoT technology. Water and nutrient solution are circulated through four horizontal PVC pipes in a zigzag arrangement, automated by an ESP32 microcontroller coordinating with a relay module and real-time sensors.

PROBLEM STATEMENT WATER CONSERVATION & REUSE

A significant amount of RO reject water generated in offices, colleges, and institutions is wasted every day. This creates an opportunity to reuse water resources through a smart hydroponic farming system that promotes water conservation and sustainable agriculture by utilizing suitable treated water for plant cultivation.

PROPOSED SOLUTION OBJECTIVES & APPROACH
  • To develop an IoT-based smart hydroponic farming system that utilizes suitable treated RO reject water for soil-less plant cultivation. The system uses an ESP32, sensors, and automated water circulation to monitor water conditions, control the pump, prevent overflow and reduce water wastage while promoting sustainable agriculture.
  • To reduce water wastage by reusing suitable RO reject water generated in offices and colleges.
  • To develop a soil-less hydroponic farming system using four horizontal PVC pipes.

Project Objectives

  • Develop an automated hydroponic system that reduces manual monitoring and labor.
  • Control water circulation based on real-time root-zone moisture readings.
  • Monitor nutrient solution temperature continuously using a waterproof probe.
  • Accommodate approximately 20 plants (5 per pipe) using four horizontal 3-inch PVC pipes arranged vertically in a zigzag 3D frame.

Mechanical Structure & Frame Finishing

  • Frame Dimensions: Rectangular MS square-pipe frame measuring 4 × 4 × 2 ft (4' width × 4' height × 2' depth) fabricated with welded joints.
  • Pipe Arrangement: Four 3-inch PVC pipes mounted in a zigzag arrangement (Pipe 1 Front, Pipe 2 Back, Pipe 3 Front, Pipe 4 Back) with 9-inch (228.6 mm) vertical centre-to-centre spacing.
  • Surface Finishing: Welded MS frame is ground smooth, polished, and finished with protective paint/colouring for corrosion prevention in high-humidity environments.
  • Plant Capacity: Four horizontal pipes accommodating approximately 20 plants in net pots (5 plants per pipe).
SECTION 02

Working Principle & Circulation System

Automated coordination between the ESP32 microcontroller, sensors, relay module, and submersible water pump in a closed-loop irrigation system.

STEP 01

Sensor Telemetry Acquisition

The capacitive moisture sensor positioned in the plant root zone and the waterproof DS18B20 temperature probe in the reservoir continuously acquire real-time environmental data and feed telemetry to the ESP32.

STEP 02

ESP32 Analysis & Relay Coordination

The ESP32 processes and analyses moisture and temperature data in real time. It coordinates with the submersible pump through a dedicated Relay Module, which provides electrical isolation and precise switching control.

STEP 03

Automated Irrigation & Pump Action

When moisture decreases around the plant roots and drops below the calibrated threshold, the ESP32 triggers the relay to start the water pump. Once the required moisture level is gained, the pump automatically stops.

Defined Operating Cycles & Automation Flow

Complete closed-loop coordination sequence executed by the ESP32 and Relay Module.

Closed-Loop Automation
CYCLE 01

Standby Monitoring

ESP32 continuously samples root-zone moisture and reservoir temperature. The relay module remains OPEN; pump is idle, allowing plant roots to breathe.

CYCLE 02

Moisture Deficit Detection

When moisture decreases around the plant roots and drops below calibrated threshold (≤ 40%), the ESP32 analyzes the deficit and initiates irrigation logic.

CYCLE 03

Relay Trigger & Circulation

ESP32 drives GPIO 26 HIGH to trigger the Relay Module. The relay closes, energizing the pump. Nutrient solution rises through the supply pipe into Pipe 1 and cascades across all 4 zigzag tiers.

CYCLE 04

Moisture Regained & Cut-Off

As nutrient solution reaches plant roots, moisture levels rise. Once target moisture is reached (≥ 65%), ESP32 opens the relay to stop the pump. Excess solution returns to reservoir.

SECTION 03

Electronics, Sensors & Control Logic

The hardware components used in our system and their automated control logic.

CONTROLLER

ESP32 NodeMCU

38-Pin WROOM-32 · Dual-Core 240MHz

Central IoT processing unit executing real-time ADC telemetry sampling, closed-loop hysteresis logic, and automated pump relay output control.

RELAY SWITCH

5V Relay Module

JQC-3F-05VDC-C · 10A 250VAC Switching

Provides optical galvanic isolation between the sensitive 3.3V microcontroller logic and the high-voltage submersible pump circuit.

MOISTURE SENSING

Moisture Sensor & Comparator

HW-103 v1.2 / LM393 · GPIO 34 (ADC)

Dual-probe sensor positioned in the top growth pipe to acquire continuous root-zone dielectric moisture saturation readings.

THERMAL TELEMETRY

DS18B20 Temperature Probe

1-Wire Digital Bus · GPIO 04 (±0.5°C)

Stainless steel encapsulated submersible probe continuously monitoring nutrient solution temperature in the reservoir bucket.

HYDRAULIC ACTUATOR

Submersible Water Pump

TU 18W · 1100 L/h · Hmax 1.8m · IPX8

High-lift continuous-duty hydraulic pump providing pressurized nutrient solution circulation from the reservoir up through the vertical riser to Pipe 1.

RESERVOIR SAFETY

Water Level Sensor (HW-038)

HW-038 · GPIO 35 (ADC) · Run-Dry Guard

Measures reservoir fluid depth using parallel conducting traces. Automatically interlocks and halts pump actuation if reservoir water drops below safety levels to prevent pump burnout.

ESP32 & Relay Coordination Architecture

Automated coordination sequence between sensors, microcontroller analysis, relay switching, and pump actuation.

Closed-Loop Control

1. Sensor Data Acquisition & Real-Time Analysis

The ESP32 microcontroller continuously reads real-time environmental telemetry from the root zone and reservoir:

  • Capacitive Moisture Sensor (GPIO 34): Monitors moisture saturation around the plant root zone in the grow pipes.
  • DS18B20 Temperature Probe (GPIO 04): Continuously tracks nutrient solution temperature in the reservoir bucket.
  • Water Level Sensor HW-038 (GPIO 35): Continuously monitors buffer reservoir liquid level depth to implement dry-run pump safety interlocks.
  • Microcontroller Analysis: ESP32 algorithms process raw ADC telemetry to detect moisture depletion and evaluate safety thresholds.

2. Relay Module Switching & Pump Action

The ESP32 coordinates directly with the submersible pump through the Relay Module:

  • Pump Activation: When moisture falls below the calibrated threshold and water level is safe, ESP32 drives GPIO 26 HIGH, closing the relay contacts to start the pump.
  • Pump Cut-Off: When optimal root moisture is gained, ESP32 drives GPIO 26 LOW, opening the relay to cut pump power and allow root oxygenation.
  • Run-Dry Safety Interlock: If HW-038 level sensor detects low reservoir volume, the ESP32 locks out pump actuation to prevent pump cavitation and dry burnout.
  • Galvanic Isolation: The optocoupler relay module protects the ESP32 from inductive kickback voltages generated by the water pump motor.

SolidWorks Mechanical & Hydraulic Specifications

Engineering parameters verified against the physical fabricated prototype.

Verified Assembly Data

Mechanical Frame & Growth Channels

  • Frame Dimensions: Rectangular MS square-pipe frame measuring 4 × 4 × 2 ft (4' width × 4' height × 2' depth), fabricated with welded joints, polished, and finished with protective colouring.
  • Growth Pipe Geometry: Four horizontal 3-inch PVC pipes mounted in a vertical zigzag arrangement across the frame.
  • Vertical Pitch: Recommended vertical centre-to-centre spacing of 9 inches (228.6 mm) between adjacent pipes.
  • Alternating Alignment: Pipe 1 at front, Pipe 2 at back, Pipe 3 at front, and Pipe 4 at back for structural rigidity and plant spacing.

Hydraulic Architecture & Component Layout

  • Plant Capacity: 5 net cup apertures per pipe (20 plants total), fitted with net pots for plant cultivation.
  • Right Reservoir Bucket: Nutrient solution container positioned on the floor to the right of the stand frame.
  • Riser Supply Pipe: Straight orthogonal orange supply pipe routing nutrient solution from the pump up to Pipe 1 top inlet.
  • Inter-Tier Elbows & Drain: Inter-pipe elbow hose loops transferring fluid between tiers, with a horizontal straight drain pipe returning effluent directly to the bucket.
